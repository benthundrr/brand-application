06 — TAP Campaigns
Commission-based creator distribution.

A TAP campaign is a commission-based affiliate product distribution option built around TikTok Affiliate Partnership agency campaign links. It helps test creator interest, increase sample requests, and get products in front of affiliates without a flat-fee commitment upfront.

TAP works best for brands that can provide samples, offer a strong creator commission, and want a lower-friction option to test creator appetite. TAP can also run as support inside flat-fee campaign packages.

Scope of Work
Agency campaign link setup
Product distribution to affiliates
Creator sample request handling
Product showcase additions
Creator interest testing
Newsletter and Discord exposure
Commission tracking
Important Note
TAP tests interest — it doesn't guarantee posts.
TAP campaigns do not guarantee creator posts, sales, or specific video volume. Best used for affiliate exposure, product distribution, and creator interest testing ahead of a larger campaign commitment.
